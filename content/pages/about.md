**C++ Tips** is somewhere in between a personal C++ blog and a collection of cool tips and tricks.
Ocassionally you might find guest articles by some of my friends.

On this site you can find:

- Short-form **tips** that seemed useful enough to share but not interesting enough for a long-form article
- Long-form **articles** about interesting C++ things
- **Updates** about noteworthy things that are happening

This is an independent site. It is not affiliated with the ISO C++ committee,
a compiler project, or any organization.
Individual posts only reflect the opinions of the respective author(s).

## About the author
### Matthias Wippich
I’m a member of the C++ committee (formally ISO/IEC JTC1/SC22/WG21) and IEEE. In my little free time I sporadically contribute to the LLVM project/clang and do a lot of gardening. I also run a [C++ Community](https://discord.com/invite/byZvFu7d94) on the Discord platform.

I used to study games engineering at [TUM](https://www.tum.de/) and am currently pursuing a degree in computational linguistics at [LMU](https://www.lmu.de/). To finance all this, I'm also working in the embedded firmware team of a life sciences and lab automation company.

My main interests are in metaprogramming, embedded and compilers. I also do quite enjoy messing around with [Python](https://pydong.org).
